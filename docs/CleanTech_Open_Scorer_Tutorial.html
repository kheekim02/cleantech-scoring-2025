1 Getting Started & Logging In

As a CleanTech Open judge, you have been assigned specific cleantech startups to evaluate. Your evaluations determine accelerator cohort admissions, awards, and investment readiness.

  1. Navigate to the scoring portal at /index.html.
  2. In the Judge Authentication modal, enter:
    • Scorer ID: Your assigned username (e.g., judge_smith or J-001).
    • Passcode: Your individual access passcode provided by the administrator.
  3. Click Sign In.
  4. In the top navigation header, click the Company Dropdown to view the startups assigned to you. Select the startup you wish to evaluate to load its application workspace.
Auto-Save Security: Every score selection and justification text box is automatically saved to the cloud the moment you click or pause typing. You can safely reload the page, switch categories, or return later without losing any work.

3 The AI Due Diligence Copilot & Citation Auto-Jumping

Every criterion has been pre-analyzed by our high-conviction clean AI extraction engine. The AI does not replace your expertise; it highlights verbatim founder claims and surfaces key passages instantly.

Anatomy of an Evaluation Card

  • Question & Criteria: The exact scoring question formulated by the CleanTech Open national curriculum.
  • AI Suggestion Pill: Displays the AI's predicted score (e.g. Val: 1.0 PT) alongside an empirical confidence score (e.g. 95% Conf).
  • Point Buttons: Standardized score radio buttons. Click any button to record your score.
  • "View AI Citation (p. X)" Link: The core diligence accelerator. Clicking this link triggers two actions:
    1. Expands the yellow citation box displaying the exact verbatim sentence from the founder's submission.

4 Scoring Philosophy & Human Overrides

Rule 1 — You Are the Ultimate Authority: The AI Copilot is calibrated to identify explicit keywords and founder claims. If you discover that a claim is unvalidated, technologically dubious, or contradicts another deliverable, override the AI score immediately.

When is Human Justification Required?

To maintain scoring integrity for founders, human written justifications are mandatory for:

  • All 5 questions in the Business Canvas (BC) category.
  • Key subjective pivot questions across Financials (F), Tech Validation (TP), and Investor Pitch (IP).
  • Any evaluation where you dramatically downgrade an AI suggestion from 1.0 PT to 0.0 PT.

A yellow text area labeled "Human Justification / Diligence Notes" will appear automatically. Enter 1 to 2 concise sentences explaining the rationale behind your verdict.

5 Step-by-Step Diligence Checklist

Follow this standardized 5-step workflow for every startup assigned to you:

Step Action Description & Best Practice
Step 1 Executive Summary Start in the Executive Summary (IS) category to read the founder's 1-page overview and understand the core thesis.
Step 2 Category Progression Progress systematically through the 10 categories using the left navigation sidebar. Watch the progress ring fill as criteria are answered.
Step 3 Inspect Citations On each question, click "View AI Citation" to verify the founder's claim against the embedded PDF deliverable on the right.
Step 4 Record Score & Notes Select your point verdict. If the question requires justification or if you disagree with the AI, type your brief explanation.
Step 5 Final Verification Ensure all 10 categories show 100% completion in the sidebar. When finished, switch to the next assigned company in the header dropdown.

6 Scorer FAQs & Pro-Tips

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What if the AI Citation says "No direct evidence exists"?
A: This means the startup's submitted deliverables did not address this specific criterion. If you independently search the document and cannot locate evidence, award 0 PTS.

Q: Can I view the official rubric scoring guidelines?
A: Yes! On questions with official rubric guidelines, click the "📄 View Examples" link in the question card to view rubric thresholds and real-world grading examples.

Q: How do I zoom or download a startup PDF?
A: The live PDF viewer on the right includes full browser controls in the top toolbar: zoom in/out (+/-), page jump, and a direct download icon to inspect the file in your preferred PDF reader.

Q: What if I notice a document misclassification?
A: All deliverables are mapped across category tabs above the PDF viewer. If you need to view a document filed under another category (e.g. Financial Projection while reviewing Business Canvas), simply click that category's tab at the top of the viewer.

Q: How long should each startup take?
A: Thanks to citation auto-jumping, an evaluation typically takes 45 to 60 minutes per startup.
