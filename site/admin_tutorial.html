CleanTech Open 2025: Administrator Operations Manual

Complete guide to managing judge accounts, overseeing startup assignments, and monitoring evaluation progress across the 2025 cohort.

Target: Program Directors & Diligence Leads Portal URL: https://cleantech-scoring-2025.vercel.app/admin.html Version: 2.4 (Clean AI Release)

1 Overview & Diligence Architecture

The CleanTech Open Diligence Platform coordinates the multi-judge evaluation of 92 cleantech startups across 10 evaluation categories (282 standardized criteria). The platform features an AI Due Diligence Copilot and live PDF viewer with auto-citation indexing to accelerate judge reviews while enforcing strict accountability.

Platform Key Components

  • Admin Portal (https://cleantech-scoring-2025.vercel.app/admin.html): Dedicated dashboard for operations leads to create judges, assign companies, and monitor completion rates in real time.
  • Scorer Portal (https://cleantech-scoring-2025.vercel.app/index.html): Focused side-by-side workspace where judges examine company deliverables on the left and enter scores with AI guidance on the right.
  • PostgreSQL Database (Supabase): Stores judge rosters, encrypted passcodes, assignment mappings, and question reviews with instant transactional auto-saving.
  • Clean AI Copilot Engine: Advanced reasoning model (Qwen 35B) running on sanitized founder text with 0% competition scaffolding and 92.7% PDF page-indexed citations.

2 Accessing the Admin Dashboard

  1. Navigate to https://cleantech-scoring-2025.vercel.app/admin.html in any modern desktop browser (Google Chrome, Safari, or Microsoft Edge recommended).

3 Managing Judges & Reviewers

Judges only have access to startups explicitly assigned to them. Administrators can quickly onboard new judges and view active reviewer credentials on the left side of the dashboard.

How to Create a New Judge

  1. Locate the Create New Scorer card on the left panel.
  2. Enter a memorable, unique Scorer ID. Recommended convention:
    • judge_smith or judge_chen (Named judges)
    • J-001, J-002, J-003 (Numbered panel IDs)
  3. Enter a secure Passcode (e.g., 4-digit code or memorable passphrase).
  4. Click Create Scorer. The judge is instantly registered in Supabase and appears in the Existing Scorers roster below.
  5. Provide the judge with the live scoring portal URL (https://cleantech-scoring-2025.vercel.app/index.html), their Scorer ID, and their passcode.
Security & Credential Recovery: All active judge IDs and their passcodes are displayed in the "Existing Scorers" scrollable box. If a judge misplaces their passcode, you can read it directly from this panel without needing to reset their database account.

4 Assigning Startups to Judges

The assignment workflow gives administrators full control over distribution, ensuring dual-reviewer consensus and preventing accidental assignment of incomplete applications.

CleanTech Open Admin Portal — Company Assignments dashboard with reviewer selection, status filters, and real-time cohort tracking.

Step-by-Step Assignment Protocol

  1. Select a Scorer: Choose the target judge from the "Select Scorer to Assign Companies" dropdown. The list immediately refreshes to reflect that judge's assignments.
  2. Use the "✓ Ready for Assignment" Filter: Always keep this filter active. It restricts the visible list to startups that have 100% completed clean AI extraction and PDF citation reverse-indexing.
  3. Assign or Unassign Companies: Check the box next to any startup to immediately assign it to the selected judge. Unchecking removes the assignment. Every toggle commits to the database instantly.
  4. Track Cohort Progress: Next to each assigned company, observe the judge's completion status:
    • Not Started: The judge has not answered any questions yet.
    • X Answers: Number of rubric questions answered so far (out of 282).

5 Understanding Assignment Badges & Distribution

To ensure high statistical validity, each startup should ideally receive two independent evaluations. The admin dashboard provides visual cues to guide assignment balancing:

The Assignment Distribution Bar Chart

Directly beneath the scorer roster, the dashboard renders a real-time bar chart illustrating the overall state of the cohort:

  • Yellow Bar (Unassigned): Count of startups with zero reviewers. Aim to reduce this to 0.
  • Orange Bar (1 Assigned): Count of startups with only 1 reviewer. These require a second assignment.
  • Green Bar (Fully Assigned 2+): Count of startups with two or more assigned judges.

6 Administrative FAQ & Operational Rules

Recommended Reviewer Workload

We recommend assigning 3 to 5 startups per judge. Each startup evaluation requires careful diligence. Overloading reviewers beyond 6 startups increases fatigue and decreases qualitative justification quality.

How Do I Reset a Scorer's Password or Delete an Account?

In the Existing Scorers panel, each reviewer has two administrative actions:

  • Reset: Click Reset to set a new password for the judge. This immediately invalidates active sessions and requires the judge to log in with the new credential.
  • Delete: Click Delete to permanently remove a test or decommissioned reviewer. Deleting a scorer cleanly removes their assignments and review sessions.

How Do I Export Scored Cohort Data?

Click the green 📥 Export Scored Data (CSV) button in the top navigation header of the Admin Portal. Your browser will download a complete CSV containing applicant names, judge IDs, rubric categories, question prompts, human scores, qualitative justifications, AI suggestions, confidence ratings, concordance flags, and citation source files.

Can Judges See Other Judges' Scores?

No. The scoring portal is strictly double-blind. Judges only see the AI suggestions, founder deliverables, and their own scoring answers. They cannot view who else is reviewing the startup or what other judges have scored.
