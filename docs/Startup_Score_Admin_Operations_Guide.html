Startup Score Admin Operations Guide

Use the Admin Portal to create scorer accounts, manage company assignments, monitor assignment progress, and keep the scoring operation healthy.

Access and dashboard orientation

  1. Open the current Admin Portal link and sign in with the administrator credentials.
  2. The left panel is for creating scorers, reviewing existing scorer IDs, and viewing assignment distribution.
  3. The right panel is for assigning companies to a selected scorer and monitoring their assignment status.
  4. Use Log Out when you are finished, especially on a shared device.

Create and manage scorer accounts

Create a scorer

  1. Enter a unique Scorer ID and a temporary password.
  2. Select Create Scorer.
  3. Give the scorer their ID, temporary password, and the current scorer portal link through an approved private channel.
  4. Ask the scorer to use Change password after signing in.

Important: the portal does not display stored scorer passwords. Passwords are protected, and an existing password cannot be retrieved from the Admin Portal.

Assign and unassign companies

  1. Select a scorer from Select Scorer to Assign Companies.
  2. Use the search field to find a company by name.
  3. Use All, Assigned, and Unassigned to narrow the company list.
  4. Check a company box to assign it; clear the box to remove that scorer's assignment.
  5. Confirm the assignment date and elapsed time shown beneath assigned companies when available.

Assignment distribution

The chart groups companies by the number of scorers assigned:

  • Unassigned: no scorer is assigned.
  • Assigned (1): one scorer is assigned.
  • Fully Assigned (2+): two or more scorers are assigned.

Review the chart before adding assignments so work is distributed evenly and companies receive the intended review coverage.

Operational limits and good practice

Do not treat the portal as a credential vault. It can create scorer accounts and show scorer IDs, but it does not reveal existing passwords or provide a general-purpose password-recovery screen.
  • Do not enter passwords in spreadsheets, task notes, or version-controlled files.
  • Before removing an assignment, confirm whether the scorer needs continued access. Existing saved reviews remain in the database unless separately removed.
  • Use the answer count for triage, then communicate with scorers when a company remains incomplete.
  • After any interface release, test administrator sign-in, scorer creation, assignment changes, scorer sign-in, saving, and submission.

Supabase backup and inactivity routine

Why this matters: a free-tier Supabase service may pause after 90 days of inactivity. Maintain recoverable exports and perform a read-only health check at least once every 30 days.
  1. Once each month, export the production database and store the encrypted export in the team-controlled backup location.
  2. Retain at least 12 monthly backups and one annual archive.
  3. Record the export date, owner, database project, and restore-test result in the operations log.
  4. Each quarter, restore a backup into a non-production project and verify that assignments and reviews are present.
