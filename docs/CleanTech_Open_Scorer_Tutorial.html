1 Getting Started & Logging In

As a CleanTech Open judge, you have been assigned specific cleantech startups to evaluate. Your evaluations determine accelerator cohort admissions, awards, and investment readiness.

  1. Navigate to the scoring portal at /index.html.
  2. In the Judge Authentication modal, enter:
    • Scorer ID: Your assigned username (e.g., judge_smith or J-001).
    • Passcode: Your individual access passcode provided by the administrator.
  3. Click Sign In.
  4. In the top dark navigation bar, click the Company Dropdown to view the startups assigned to you. Select the startup you wish to evaluate to load its application workspace.
Cloud Auto-Save: Every score selection and justification note is automatically saved to the Supabase database the moment you click a score or pause typing. You can safely reload the page or return on another device without losing work.

3 The AI Copilot & The Mandatory Human Review Rule

⚠️ CRITICAL PROTOCOL: Human Review is Mandatory Regardless of AI Confidence

Never rubber-stamp high-confidence AI suggestions. An AI confidence rating of 90%, 95%, or 100% does NOT mean the startup's claim is commercially viable, technically sound, or factually true in the real world.

  • What AI Confidence Actually Measures: The AI confidence score solely reflects how clearly an explicit textual statement was found in the founder's uploaded text. If a founder writes "Our technology has zero competitors and 100% customer retention", the AI will extract a high-confidence match (1.0 PT | 95% Conf).
  • What the AI Cannot Do: The AI cannot verify whether the founder's claim is realistic, whether lab data supports it, whether customer interviews were genuine, or whether the competitive analysis is superficial.
  • Your Role as an Expert Judge: You must click the citation link, review the cited passage in the PDF viewer on the left, inspect the surrounding context, and determine whether the startup genuinely satisfied the standard. You have full authority to override the AI whenever founder evidence lacks depth or credibility.

Deliverables, Sourcing & External Research

Q: Can I Google search if I cannot find information in the startup's submitted files?
A: Yes. You are explicitly authorized and encouraged to Google search the company, visit their website, inspect founder LinkedIn profiles, and review public press releases or patent filings if a question cannot be answered from their submitted files.

Q: What if a company has files but no dedicated Business Model Canvas (BMC) document?
A: Review their other submitted deliverables (e.g. Investor Pitch Deck, Executive Summary, Customer Discovery log) and web search to evaluate their business model as best as possible. Award 0 PTS only if no business model information exists across all materials.

Q: What if a company has missing files or access is blocked?
A: If all materials for a company are completely missing, notify the administrator. If an application survey text exists, evaluate based on that text. For any question where no evidence exists anywhere, award 0 PTS.

Q: Which should I score if both Application Phase and EBD Phase versions exist?
A: Always evaluate the Essential Business Deliverables (EBD) Phase files. EBD represents the finalized, post-program deliverables (e.g. validated financial models, customer interview sheets). Only reference early Application Phase survey answers if an EBD document is absent.

Module Scoping, Jargon & Scoring Scale

Q: Are question categories strictly scoped to specific deliverables, or can I cross-reference?
A:

  • Strict Scoping: Business Canvas (BC), Executive Summary (IS), and Investor Pitch (IP) must be evaluated primarily on their respective deliverables (EBD1, EBD6, EBD8).
  • Flexible Scoping: For all other categories (Financials, Tech Validation, Market, Product-Market Fit, Team, Legal, Environmental Impact), you are free to cross-reference any submitted file or online source to verify founder assertions.

Q: How should I score technical buzzwords or industry jargon?
A: 1–2 technical or domain-specific terms used to explain their core innovation are acceptable. However, if the submission relies on excessive, unexplained buzzwords that obscure their real business model or technology, penalize their clarity score.

Q: Do I need to calculate weights, averages, or formula scores?
A: No. In historical spreadsheets, reviewers had to deal with complex decimal weights. The modern Diligence Engine standardizes all 282 criteria onto a clean 0 to 1 point scale (0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1.0 PTS). The platform handles all aggregation, category rollups, and cohort scoring automatically.

Q: Why do some questions show legacy numbers like (1-5) or (0.44) in the prompt?
A: These numbers are preserved from the original rubric questions for provenance. Simply select the appropriate score button on the standardized 0 – 1 point scale based on the criteria thresholds.

Q: Should I recalibrate my scores after finishing?
A: Yes. Once you complete scoring all assigned startups, take 5 minutes to review your score distribution across your cohort to ensure your evaluation rigor remained consistent from your first company to your last.

Platform Capabilities & Persistence

Q: What if the AI Citation says "No direct evidence exists"?
A: This means the AI did not locate explicit text matching this criterion. If you independently search the document and cannot locate evidence, award 0 PTS.

Q: Can I view the official rubric scoring guidelines?
A: Yes! On questions with official rubric guidelines (e.g. Business Canvas), click the "📄 View Examples" link in the question card to view rubric thresholds and real-world grading examples calibrated directly to the 0 – 1 point scale.

Q: What happens if I close my browser before clicking Submit?
A: All scores and justifications are continuously saved to the Supabase database in real time. When you reopen the portal, all your answers will automatically reload from the database.
